Privacy policy

AyniteDM download manager and the AyniteDM browser add-on, by AyniteSoft. Last updated 8 October 2026.

The short version

AyniteDM and its browser add-on work entirely on your own computer. They do not send your browsing data, links, files, cookies or personal information to AyniteSoft or to anyone else.

What the browser add-on does with data

  • Links and media addresses on the pages you visit are read so the add-on can show a Download button on videos, offer "Download with AyniteDM" in the right-click menu, and list media found on the page. They are sent only to the AyniteDM program running on the same computer (over the local address 127.0.0.1).
  • Cookies for a page are passed to the AyniteDM program on the same computer only when you start a download from that page and only if the "Send cookies for protected files" option is on. This lets AyniteDM download files and videos that need you to be signed in. You can switch it off in the add-on popup.
  • Browser downloads are handed to AyniteDM only when the "Capture browser downloads" option is on.
  • The add-on stores its own settings (port, options) in your browser's extension storage. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

What the AyniteDM program does with data

  • Your download list, settings and saved site logins are kept in your Windows user profile. Saved passwords are encrypted with Windows Data Protection, bound to your Windows account.
  • The program connects to the websites you download from, to the video sites you ask it to download from, and to the AyniteDM update feed to check for new versions. It downloads the video engine's updates from the public Python package index.
  • No usage statistics, crash reports or identifiers are collected or transmitted.

Third-party services

Downloads from video sites, cloud storage providers, torrent peers and other servers are subject to those services' own terms and privacy policies.

Children

AyniteDM is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.
